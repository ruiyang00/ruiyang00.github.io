<!-- I’m a 5th-year PhD candidate at the University of Virginia.  -->
The primary aim of my research is to design practical, efficient, and user-friendly infrastructure systems that support AI applications. My research interests lie in operating systems, distributed systems, and storage systems, with a current focus on uncovering the new potentials when the system invlves large scale processing, machine learning and storage. My current work focuses on: (1) **ML-enhanced storage systems**: improving database performance using workload-aware learned components; and (2) **Serverless LLM inference systems**: addressing cold-start latency, GPU resource allocation, and LLM scaling.
